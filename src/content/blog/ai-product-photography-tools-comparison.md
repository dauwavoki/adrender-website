Flair and Pebblely solve a real, narrow problem well: turning a flat product photo into something that looks professionally shot, without a photographer or a studio. If that's genuinely all you need, either is a reasonable tool. But it's worth being precise about what they do and don't do, because "product photo" and "ad" are not the same deliverable.

**Flair** gives you a drag-and-drop canvas — you place your product into a scene, adjust lighting and props, and build the composition by hand. It's more creative control than most alternatives, with a genuine free tier and paid plans starting around $8–10/month. The tradeoff is time: every image is a manual composition, not a generated batch.

**Pebblely** automates the same idea further — upload a product photo, pick from 40+ preset background themes (or describe one), and it generates the scene. Faster than Flair, but the themes are shared across every Pebblely user, so results can start to look familiar across sellers in the same niche. Worth knowing before you commit: Pebblely's "free" tier is 40 images total, one time — not a monthly allowance like most freemium tools. Paid plans run $15–32/month depending on volume.

**Neither tool writes ad copy, builds a layout, or knows what's actually converting in your niche right now.** You still need headlines, a CTA, sizing for whatever platform you're running on, and some sense of what format is actually working. A beautifully lit product photo sitting alone on a blank page isn't an ad — it's a nicer product photo.
