AdScale and Madgicx get mentioned in the same breath as "AI ad tools for Shopify" a lot, but they're solving a meaningfully different problem than a pure creative generator. Both are built to manage your ad accounts — bidding, budgets, targeting — with creative generation as a feature bolted onto that core job, not the main event.

**AdScale** connects directly to your Shopify store and runs campaigns across Google and Meta on your behalf, adjusting bids and budgets continuously. It's a genuinely full-service platform — some sellers describe it as replacing an agency. That comes at a price that scales with how much you're spending on ads: plans start around $149/month for up to $1,000 in monthly spend, and climb from there as your spend tier increases. There's no free tier, only a 14-day trial.

**Madgicx** is narrower in scope — Meta only, meaning Facebook and Instagram. If any part of your strategy touches Google, TikTok, or elsewhere, Madgicx's automation doesn't extend there. Its creative tools generate mostly static images and edits from your existing ads or product pages; it's not a from-scratch creative engine so much as a way to remix what you already have. Pricing also scales with ad spend, starting around $45–99/month.

**The core question to ask before picking either one:** do you actually want a platform managing your media buying, or do you want the creative and to keep control of where and how it runs? If you already have a media buyer, an in-house person, or you're comfortable running campaigns yourself, paying a spend-tiered platform to also manage bidding is solving a problem you don't have.
