If you've searched "AI ad generator" in the last year, you've run into AdCreative.ai, Predis, and Quickads. All three promise the same thing: skip the designer, skip the agency, get ads out the door fast. They're not the same tool wearing different skins, though — each one optimizes for a different part of the problem, and understanding which part matters for figuring out if any of them actually solve yours.

**AdCreative.ai** leans hardest into "AI decides for you." It scores creatives before you spend a dollar on them, which sounds great in theory. In practice, a lot of Shopify sellers report the outputs feeling generic — recognizably AI-made, in the way that's become its own red flag with shoppers in 2026. Pricing starts around $39/month and scales quickly if you need volume, and video is gated behind higher tiers.

**Predis** takes a different angle: it's built primarily for social media content and scheduling, with ad generation as one feature among many. If you need a tool that also handles your organic posting calendar, that's a real advantage. If you specifically need paid ad creative — static and video built for running spend behind, not for a feed — you'll find yourself working around a tool that wasn't built for that job first.

**Quickads** is the budget option, with pricing that's genuinely hard to pin down (published numbers range from $9 to $99/month depending on where you look, and user reviews flag billing surprises around trial-end dates). It leans on a large swipe library of past ads for inspiration, which is useful, but a swipe library is a snapshot of what worked historically — not necessarily what's performing right now.

**What connects all three:** none of them are built Shopify-first. You're either manually entering product info, pasting URLs one at a time, or working from a generic product feed integration that doesn't actually sync your catalog — colors, variants, and all — the way a Shopify-native tool would.
