If you run a Shopify store, the hard part of advertising usually isn't the budget. It's the creative: finding the time, the designer and the product shots to keep fresh ads coming. The AdRender Shopify app is built to take that step off your plate, and you can [install it from the Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/adrender).

## What the AdRender Shopify app does

AdRender turns your product catalog into image and video ads for Facebook and Instagram. Install the app and your products and collections connect automatically. Choose a template modeled on ads from your category, and AdRender renders it with your products, your brand colors, your logo and your brand voice. An ad copy generator with a built-in safety check writes the text, so the ad is ready to download and launch.

## Why use the Shopify app

- **It starts from your catalog.** Your products and collections connect when you install, so you aren't uploading product photos one by one.
- **Install and billing stay in Shopify.** Find it in the App Store, install it like any other app, and approve your plan in Shopify. Your plan is billed through your Shopify account, with no separate card to set up.
- **Unused tokens roll over.** On paid plans, unused plan tokens carry into the next billing cycle, up to twice your monthly allowance, so a quiet month doesn't go to waste.
- **Images and video from the same product.** Make still and video ads from one product, without a second tool or a film shoot.
- **On-brand by default.** Colors, logo and voice carry over to every ad, and you can manage more than one brand in one account.
- **Your own assets too.** You can scan a website or upload your own images and brand assets whenever you want to.
- **Free to start, no watermarks.** Try it on your own products before you commit to anything.

## How to get started

1. Open the [AdRender page on the Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/adrender) and install the app.
2. Let your products connect.
3. Choose a template.
4. Render your ads, then download them and launch.

## Shopify app or the web app?

AdRender is also available as a web app at [adrender.app](https://app.adrender.app/signup), and the two work in a similar way. You can sign in to the web app with the same email you used for the Shopify app. By default, each keeps its own plan and tokens.

Choose the Shopify app if your store is on Shopify and you'd like billing and installation handled there. The web app is the better fit if you want team workspaces, token top-ups or the affiliate program. You can compare plans on the [pricing page](https://adrender.app/#pricing).
